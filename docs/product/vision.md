# Vision

## What

A mobile app where a university student picks the courses she is taking and, for each one, sees the course's grading tree (final grade → criteria → evaluations). She records grades with a slider (integers 0–20) and every parent recalculates instantly, so she can play with "what if I get X in the final exam?".

## For whom

v1: one real student in Peru with 7 courses. Later: her classmates.

## Peruvian grading context

- Scale 0–20. Passing is 10.5 or more (10.5 rounds to 11).
- Each course has its own weights, published in the syllabus.

## What it is NOT (v1)

- No accounts, no backend, no sync. Everything stays on the phone.
- No course editor for students. Courses ship as templates in the app.
- No decimal input. Only the slider, integers.
- No AI features.

## Success

She opens the app before an exam and, in under 10 seconds, knows the minimum grade she needs.
